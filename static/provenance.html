What this record is worth

The section labels are a declaration — a good-faith statement of how you worked, like citing your sources. A tool can't verify them, and this one doesn't pretend to.

The draft comparison is evidence. If you paste your own earlier draft, the numbers ("82% of the final text is word-for-word from my draft") are computed from the two texts and are hard to fake — that is what actually answers an accusation, especially if you used AI only for grammar, spelling, or dictation.
