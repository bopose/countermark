What this tool can and cannot tell you

It can find and show you hidden characters that are really in the text: zero-width spaces, direction-control characters, look-alike letters from other alphabets, odd spaces, and other non-printing marks. These sit at exact positions and are shown below.

It cannot detect a statistical "text watermark" of the kind AI vendors embed by subtly biasing word choice. Those can only be confirmed by the vendor that made them, using a secret key. No local tool can find one, and any tool that claims to is really guessing from writing style — the same unreliable guess that unfairly flags many non-native and neurodiverse writers.

So: finding nothing here does not prove text is watermark-free, and finding something here does not prove it was AI-generated. This tool shows you what is verifiably present. Nothing more, nothing less.
